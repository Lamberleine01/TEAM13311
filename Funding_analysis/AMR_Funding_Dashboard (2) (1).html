AMR R&D Funding Analysis — Nigeria, Africa & Global, viewed through a disability-inclusion lens

Source: Global AMR R&D Hub exports · VIVLI Data Project · Data Analytics Unit

Core finding: Across every export used in this dashboard (by country, funder, research area, infectious agent, product stage, year) the Global AMR R&D Hub contains no field disaggregating funding by disability status. This dashboard cannot show "AMR funding for persons with disabilities in Nigeria" because that category does not exist in the underlying data — which is itself the central finding for the project's funding workstream.
